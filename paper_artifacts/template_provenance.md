# Manuscript format

The public preprint uses the unmodified official ICML 2026 style with its
`preprint` option. This is the latest verified author template at the time of
revision; it does not claim that this work was submitted to or accepted by ICML.
The author information and public repository links remain appropriate for a
public preprint. An actual submission would require the target year's rules and
anonymization.

Source: https://media.icml.cc/Conferences/ICML2026/Styles/icml2026.zip
