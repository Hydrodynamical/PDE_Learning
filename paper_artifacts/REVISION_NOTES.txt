TECHNICAL AUDIT NOTE: LEGACY SENSITIVITY SCORES
2 November 2026 - moved out of former Appendix E at the author's request.
These diagnostics do not support any figure or result in the current paper.
Current confidence analyses compare reported probabilities with actual
coefficient correctness. The code-audit explanation is retained here for
provenance; the original research repository has not been changed.

Source: main_loop.py,
compute_metacognitive_metrics() (starting at line 1158).
Older diagnostic plots: plot_hackathon.py,
_draw_metacog_conf_sigma(), _draw_metacog_control(), and plot_metacognition().

The archived routine forms a truncated pseudoinverse H_k of A_k^T A_k,
discarding singular values below 10^-10 times the largest. For a coefficient
block q with N basis coefficients, it calculates
s_k(q) = sqrt(sum_{i in q}(H_k)_{ii} / N).
A truncated inverse can assign zero weight to unresolved directions. Without
an observation-noise model and rank conditions, it is not a calibrated
coefficient covariance. Separate block values also cannot diagnose dependencies
between blocks.

The monitoring routine ranks values using double argsort, without averaging
ties; it is not standard tie-aware Spearman correlation. The control routine
applies a Sherman-Morrison-style update to a truncated pseudoinverse, which
need not agree with a freshly recomputed truncated inverse, and compares
reductions across blocks for the chosen next row rather than across candidate
queries. These source fields are retained for provenance but are not used as
evidence of calibrated uncertainty or optimal experimental design.
